# Art & Visual References: Comic + Light + Twist

Visual research for a 15-minute web game combining **Comic-book look**, **Light mechanics**, and a **Gameplay/Narrative Twist**.

---

### 1. Comix Zone (Sega Genesis)
- **Reference:** The classic 2D action title where the protagonist is trapped inside the inked panels of his own comic book.
- **What to borrow:** Panel borders serving as physical platforms/walls, gutters as hazards/transitions, and comic onomatopoeia ("POW!", "WHAM!") reacting to actions.

### 2. Framed & Framed 2 (Loveshack Entertainment)
- **Reference:** Noir narrative puzzle game told across silhouetted comic book panels rearranged by the player to alter outcomes.
- **What to borrow:** High-contrast silhouette characters set against bold solid color washes, and panel arrangement that flips cause-and-effect (the twist).

### 3. Liberated (Atomic Wolf)
- **Reference:** A hand-drawn dystopian graphic novel game featuring real-time black-and-white comic panels illuminated by handheld flashlights.
- **What to borrow:** Dynamic light cones cutting through heavy crosshatching and halftone textures, guiding player attention across inked comic frames.

### 4. Viewtiful Joe (Capcom)
- **Reference:** Cel-shaded superhero beat-'em-up with dynamic halftone dots, bold ink contours, and visual sound-effect graphics.
- **What to borrow:** Ben-Day dot shading patterns, punchy halftone overlays, and comic-style impact banners that pop into view during key moments.

### 5. Iris.Fall (NEXT Studios)
- **Reference:** Monochromatic puzzle-adventure using stark light and shadow projection where 2D shadows become physical pathways.
- **What to borrow:** High-contrast lighting that transforms passable terrain into void or reveals hidden walkable geometry only when lit.

### 6. Alan Wake 2 (The Dark Place / Light-Shift)
- **Reference:** Atmospheric noir where a flashlight burns away ink-like shadow shields and shifts narrative reality to reveal hidden writing.
- **What to borrow:** Focused light beams that "burn away" or reveal hidden ink entities, and light switches that transform the environment's state.

### 7. The Wolf Among Us / Telltale Comic Shaders
- **Reference:** Neo-noir comic adaptation utilizing thick black ink contours and saturated neon light sources cutting through grime.
- **What to borrow:** 3-4px heavy black contour lines on sprites and neon/amber light sources that contrast vividly against pitch-black shadows.

### 8. Inscryption (Daniel Mullins)
- **Reference:** Atmospheric tabletop horror that begins in a single eerie candlelit space before a shocking meta-twist flips the interface and mechanics.
- **What to borrow:** Intimate, localized lighting (lantern/candle) creating false comfort, subverting player trust when the UI and rules suddenly invert.

### 9. Limbo (Playdead)
- **Reference:** Iconic 2D puzzle-platformer utilizing stark silhouettes, volumetric fog, backlighting, and sudden environmental twists.
- **What to borrow:** Minimalist black silhouette foregrounds against glowing, gradient light sources; highly readable for fast web rendering.

### 10. Bendy and the Ink Machine (Joey Drew Studios)
- **Reference:** Vintage cartoon aesthetic where living ink, sepia paper, and flashlight beams expose hidden messages and hostile ink anomalies.
- **What to borrow:** Sepia parchment backgrounds, liquid ink puddles, and flashlight mechanics that uncover invisible clues before the ink turns hostile.
