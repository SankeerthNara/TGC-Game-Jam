1. Pitch & Core Concept

A fixed Narrator tells four comic stories in a row. In each one you play that comic's hero (a superhero, a noir detective, a manga ninja, a pop-art space hero) in a pitch-dark level of 8 rooms in a random layout themed to that comic: only the area around your torch is lit. Finish the level's interactive tasks to fill the progress bar. Levels take about 2, 3, 4 and 5 minutes and go easier if you run over par; a score (not just time) ranks you. The lights were cut by the comic's villain, who keeps sabotaging you with timed sabotage from hidden triggers. Two identical vampires lurk in the dark: one is your friend, the other the villain. Which is which is pure chance. When you find a vampire you must choose: REVEAL or KILL. Reveal a friend and he becomes an ally who no longer fears light; reveal the villain and the hero is teleported into a 30 to 60 second parkour chase to catch him. Kill the villain and he is gone; kill a friend and he dies. After four levels you face the Narrator himself in a final boss fight of at least 3 minutes.

2. Integration of the Three Themes

ThemeHow It Drives Gameplay & Narrative
Comic The station is a comic-book studio (ink outlines, halftone paper, narrator captions, onomatopoeia) and the light puzzles are comic pages whose panels are movable tiles; gutters act as light refractors.
Light The whole map is dark by design: only the area around your torch is visible, so light is how you explore. Light is also the puzzle: steering a beam off mirrors onto star targets.
Twist Who is the friend and who is the villain? Nobody can tell, so every choice is a gamble. The Narrator is the final twist: he is the real saboteur. In the puzzle pages the narrator also twists the rules (the hero you light turns out to be the villain, mirrors lie, gutters bend light).

3. Core Mechanics

  • Explore the dark: Smooth 8-direction walking in a seamless tile map, a new random 8-room layout every level. Only the area around the torch is lit. Press M for the station map.
  • 28 interactive tasks over 4 levels, none repeated, one progress bar per level: comic tasks (order the panels, fill speech bubbles, letter sound effects), college tasks (debug code, logic gates, shelve books), plus wires, fuses, lantern code, torch charge, telescope, ink spills, card swipe, and light-puzzle pages.
  • Villain-started sabotage and health: the villain vampire himself walks to a spot and starts a timer: 1 sabotage in level 1, 2 in level 2, 2 big ones in levels 3 and 4, and in level 4 a hidden task also undoes one of your finished tasks. A fix console must be completed in time or the hero loses hearts. Zero hearts: respawn at the level checkpoint. Par times and a total timer track your speed.
  • Light puzzle pages: drag panels to swap them (their contents move too), click mirrors to flip them, steer the beam onto every goal. Unlimited undo.
  • Vampires, Reveal or Kill: two identical vampires per level (friend and villain, random) flee from your torch. Catch one in the light and choose REVEAL (friend: ally; villain: a parkour chase) or KILL (villain dies; friend dies). The friend helps more each level.
  • Parkour chase and boss: revealing the villain starts a 30 to 60 second parkour run to catch him; the final boss fight with the Narrator lasts at least 3 minutes (three phases, mirrors, surviving allies help).
  • Beam Tracing: an instant headless raycast lights targets and flags hazards after every move.
  • Frictionless: [Z] interact/undo, [R] reset, [M] map, Esc leave a task; no softlocks.

6. Scope Boundaries

In Scope: 4 generated dark levels with torch lighting, 26 mini-game tasks with a progress bar, sabotage timers and hearts, station map, 6 tuned light-puzzle pages, 3 rule mutations, goal flip twist, saboteur story twist, narrator caption UI, menus, end screen, comic sound effects, music, web export.
Out of Scope (Strictly Cut): Level editor, save slots, mobile touch, gamepads, multiplayer, more than 20 rooms.

7. Technical Architecture & Web Feasibility

  • Engine & Renderer: Godot 4.7 with GL Compatibility renderer; single-threaded Web export with zero native extensions or GDExtensions.
  • Model-View Decoupling: pure GDScript puzzle model (PageModel, BeamSolver) separate from rendering; the station (World) and its levels and tasks are data-driven from a JSON map; each mini-game is its own class. Tested headless.
  • Automated Validation: Built-in BFS/DFS solver (level_check.gd) verifies every level is 100% solvable within 2–8 optimal moves.
  • Web Export: game package under 1 MB PCK (build about 38 MB uncompressed, ~8 MB gzip). 2D lights and darkness verified in a browser build.
