1. Pitch & Core Concept

The game opens with a cutscene: the Earth once lived in peace, guarded by four superheroes, until a masked villain covered it in darkness. A fixed Narrator then tells four comic stories in a row. Each is built for one comic hero (superhero, noir detective, manga ninja, pop-art space hero) in a pitch-dark level of 8 rooms in a random layout where only your torch is lit; you finish interactive tasks to fill a progress bar. Level 1 is a calm warm-up, and at its end the Narrator captures that hero. From level 2, two identical vampires lurk in the dark: your friend and the comic's villain (random), who starts timed sabotage. Catch one in your torchlight (an Among Us style cutscene) and choose REVEAL or KILL: reveal a friend and he becomes an ally who no longer fears light; reveal the villain and you are teleported into a 30 to 60 second parkour chase; kill the villain and he is gone; kill a friend and he dies. After four levels the heroes of levels 2, 3 and 4 fight the Narrator one on one, each with his own special power, to free the captured hero. The first two are canonically defeated, but each cracks a layer of the Narrator's power; the level 4 hero finishes it: if he wins, sunlight makes the Narrator evaporate; if not, he throws the captured hero into lava. A closing cutscene mirrors the opening: the Earth in sunlight with its heroes, or still in darkness.

2. Integration of the Three Themes

ThemeHow it drives gameplay and narrative
ComicA fixed Narrator frames four comic worlds, each with its own hero and look (ink outlines, halftone paper, caption boxes, onomatopoeia). The light puzzles are comic pages whose panels you drag around.
LightEvery level is dark: only the area around your torch is visible, so light is how you explore, how you catch vampires (they freeze when held in the beam) and how you solve the mirror puzzles and the final duels.
TwistFriend or villain? Two identical vampires, chosen at random, so every Reveal or Kill is a gamble with four different outcomes. The Narrator is the final twist: he captured the first hero and is the real saboteur. He also tampers with the interface and the puzzle rules.

4. Core Mechanics

  • Explore the dark: smooth 8-direction walking in a random 8-room layout that is new every level. Press M for the map.
  • 28 interactive tasks over 4 levels, none repeated: comic tasks (order panels, speech bubbles, sound effects, connect the dots, ink mixing), college tasks (debug code, logic gates, quiz, proofreading, safe cracking) and classics (wires, fuses, pipes, memory), plus 6 light-puzzle pages with rule twists (lying mirrors, light that bends at gutters, goal flip).
  • Villain-started sabotage and health: the villain vampire walks to a spot and starts a timer; a fix console must be completed in time or you lose hearts. Zero hearts: respawn at the level checkpoint.
  • Adaptive difficulty and score: going over par makes the next level a little easier; a score (tasks, sabotage stopped, hearts, deaths, choices, small time bonus) gives S/A/B/C ranks. Level and total timers.

5. Final Boss, Cutscenes and Endings

  • Relay duels, special powers: after level 4 the heroes of levels 2, 3 and 4 fight the Narrator one on one, in that order, at least 3 minutes in all. Each hero has his own special power: the noir detective's Deduction (slows time and shows the Narrator's next attack), the manga ninja's Light Dash (a fast dash that slices through ink), the space hero's Prism Cannon (splits and reflects a beam, and charges a Solar Flare). It is canon that the level 2 and level 3 heroes are defeated, but each cracks a layer of the Narrator's dark shield. The level 4 hero faces the weakened Narrator, and what you did earlier (friends revealed or killed, how well the first duels went) decides how hard that last duel is.
  • Two endings: if the level 4 hero wins, his Solar Flare floods the room with sunlight and the Narrator evaporates. If he loses, the Narrator throws the captured level 1 hero into lava.
  • Cutscenes: an opening cutscene (the peaceful Earth guarded by four superheroes, then covered in darkness by a masked villain) and a mirrored closing cutscene (sunlight returns, or darkness stays, depending on the ending). In between, Among Us style scenes for detecting a vampire, revealing a friend, revealing the villain (ending in a teleport to the parkour), killing a friend or the villain (ejection into space), and the level 1 capture. All are skippable.

6. Scope Boundaries

In scope: 4 generated dark levels with torch lighting, 28 tasks (never repeated), villain sabotage, hearts and checkpoints, two vampires with the Reveal / Kill choice and friend ally, parkour chase, relay boss duels, cutscenes, score and ranks, narrator and four heroes, menus, sound and music, web export.
Out of scope (strictly cut): level editor, save slots, mobile touch, gamepads, multiplayer, more than 4 levels or heroes, any existing comic character (all heroes and villains are original).
STRETCH only after everything above is playable, in this order: friends from earlier levels fighting beside the heroes, a torch battery with recharge lamps, reactive music. Cut first if time is short: stretch items, then hero abilities, then per-hero task skins (fallback: palette swaps only), then the mirror part of the duels (fallback: a torch-only duel).

7. Technical Architecture and Web Feasibility

  • Godot 4.7, GDScript only, GL Compatibility renderer: single-threaded Web export, no native extensions. 2D lights and darkness verified in a browser build.
  • Data-driven and testable: a pure-logic puzzle model with headless unit tests and a brute-force solver that proves every puzzle solvable; levels are generated at run time (8 rooms, corridors, consoles) and a generator test checks that 100+ levels are fully reachable. Every task is its own class registered by name.
  • Status at Hour 12: the whole loop already runs: levels, 26 task types plus 6 puzzle pages, sabotage, vampires with the choice, friend ally, parkour, cutscenes for every vampire moment, score, checkpoints, par times and adaptive difficulty, map screen, comic UI and menus, and a browser build.

10. Work Split and Workflow

  • Claude Code: architecture, vampire AI and choice system, duels, level generator, event scripting, bug fixing, code review and merges.
  • Antigravity: hero and villain art, level themes, UI, shaders and transitions, audio, browser (Playwright) playtests, the itch.io page.
  • ChatGPT / Codex: tests, balance data, documentation, README and CREDITS, small isolated features.
  • Developer (Sankeerth): all design decisions, the story, hero and villain concepts, the Narrator's script, the playtests, every merge to main. Each tool works in its own git worktree and owns separate folders; a shared task board tracks everything and commits are small and frequent, so the history is auditable.

11. Assets and AI Disclosure

  • Assets: no paid assets. Fonts Bangers and Comic Neue (SIL OFL 1.1); sound effects and music generated procedurally (CC0); all art is drawn in code or generated procedurally. Every asset is listed in CREDITS.md and on the itch.io page. The open-source Godot AI plugin (MIT) is used in the editor only and is not part of the game.
  • AI tools: Claude Code (architecture and gameplay code), Antigravity (UI, art and audio, browser tests) and ChatGPT/Codex (tests and documents), all logged in docs/AI_USAGE.md. All game code is written inside the 100 hours. Story, design decisions, characters and direction are the developer's own.

12. Risks and Mitigations

RiskMitigation
Scope (four heroes, duels, cutscenes)Everything shares one engine, one cutscene template and one duel engine; the written cut list; feature freeze at Hour 85.
A pure-chance choice feels unfairAll four outcomes are fun, not punishing; early playtests at Hour 30; level 1 is a safe warm-up.
Combat or parkour is not funSimple readable mechanics, playtests at Hours 45 and 70; fallbacks: shorter parkour with fewer hazards, duels without the mirror part.
Par times are missedTimed playtests tune task counts and difficulty; going over par already makes the next level easier, so the run stays near 17 to 19 minutes.
Web export or performance bugsBrowser test on every milestone, Compatibility renderer from day one, small asset budget, 2D lights limited to the torch.
AI quota limitsWork is split across three tools with handoff notes on the task board; the developer reassigns tasks to whichever tool has quota left.
